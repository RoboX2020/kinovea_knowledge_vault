# Frequently asked questions

This is a list of the most frequently asked questions about Kinovea.

## Notes in this chapter

- [[13a1 1. Can I use Kinovea on MacOS- on Linux- on Android- on iPhone-]] — No, Kinovea is only supported on Windows.
- [[13a2 2. Can I use Kinovea in my school- my university- my bike fit or shoe fit business- my physiotherapy or podiatry practice-]] — Yes, you can use Kinovea for *any* purposes, including but not limited to personal, professional, commercial, academic and research.
- [[13a3 3. What is the price of Kinovea-]] — Kinovea is a free program, it does not cost anything to use.
- [[13a4 4. Is Kinovea compliant with HIPAA or GDPR-]] — Not applicable.
- [[13a5 5. In which city is Kinovea made-]] — Not applicable.
- [[13a6 6. Which camera should I buy-]] — It is not the place of Kinovea or its authors to suggest one camera brand over another.
- [[13a7 7. Why is the speed of my video slowing down by itself-]] — The speed slider is forced down when the original frame rate of the video cannot be sustained by the player.
