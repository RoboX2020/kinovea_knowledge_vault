# Guidelines to minimize errors

The following guidelines are designed to minimize the systematic and random errors inherent to measuring spatial motion on 2D images.

## Notes in this chapter

- [[5f1 Camera setup]] — The camera must remain stationary for the images to provide a stable frame of reference.
- [[5f2 Camera and digitization]] — Use a lens with as little distortion as possible to minimize the curvature of the plane of motion in the images.
- [[5f3 Scene setup]] — The scaling object used for calibration should occupy as much of the image as possible.
