# Changing the time of a key image

To change the time of a key image you can drag its thumbnail from the bottom panel and drop it to a new position in the timeline.

Alternatively you can move the play head to the desired time, then right click the thumbnail and select **Move to current time** to set the time of the key image to the current frame in the viewport.
