# Moving camera or moving coordinates

- The [[6f Tracked coordinates|coordinate system]] or the calibration objects themselves can be tracked.

- 2D image stabilization uses a track to compensate for camera or object translation.

- To estimate more [[6g Camera motion estimation|complex camera motion]] there is a dedicated mode with its own interface.

- [[6h Camera compensated annotations|Camera compensated annotations]] stick to the real world despite camera motion.
