# Tracking of points, objects and people

- The fundamental tool of tracking is the “track”, visually represented by [[6b Trajectory object|trajectory objects]].

- Kinovea implements several [[6c Tracking algorithms|tracking algorithms]] with their own parameters.

- [[6d Trackable drawings|Trackable drawings]] like angles, polylines or markers can have tracks attached to them.

- For full body tracking, when manual digitization is too time consuming and template-based tracking fails, we can turn to [[6e Markerless tracking|markerless tracking]] using external machine learning frameworks.
